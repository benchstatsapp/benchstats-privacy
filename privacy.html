Privacy Policy

BenchStats is designed to keep your hockey data under your control.

Effective November 13, 2026

Information you add

BenchStats stores the teams, player names and numbers, schedules, game events, notes, and preferences you enter. This information provides scoring, statistics, backup, device-sync, sharing, and video-note features.

Private iCloud sync

Your BenchStats database can sync through Apple CloudKit between devices signed into the same Apple ID. This private sync is provided by Apple and is not visible to other BenchStats users.

Optional team sharing

If you choose to share a team, BenchStats sends a team snapshot—including its roster, games, events, and notes—to Apple CloudKit. People you invite can access the shared, read-only team. The owner controls the invitation and publishes updates manually.

Exports and backups

Backups and game files are created only when you choose to export or share them. You control exported files in the destination where you save or send them.

LiveBarn and Photos

LiveBarn authentication and video access occur through LiveBarn's website. BenchStats does not store your LiveBarn password. BenchStats adds clips to Photos only when you choose to save them and grant permission.

Tracking and advertising

BenchStats does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

You can delete teams, games, practices, players, notes, and presets inside the app. Deletions can sync to your other devices. Shared-team access is managed by the team owner through Apple's sharing controls.

Children's information

BenchStats is intended for coaches, team staff, and parents. Only enter player information you are authorized to manage, and avoid unnecessary personal information in notes.

Third-party services

iCloud and CloudKit are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices. BenchStats is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LiveBarn. LiveBarn use is governed by LiveBarn's terms and privacy practices.

Contact
