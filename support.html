BenchStats Support

Quick help for protecting your data and resolving common issues.

Protect your data first

Export a fresh backup from Settings and keep a copy outside BenchStats. If you need help recovering existing data, do not delete the app.

Contact support

Email benchstatsapp@gmail.com. Include the BenchStats version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a description of what happened.

Please do not email player data, exported backups, or LiveBarn credentials.

Email BenchStats Support

iCloud device sync

BenchStats syncs private app data between devices signed into the same Apple ID. In BenchStats, open Settings → My Devices to check whether iCloud is available. Changes can take a short time to appear, especially after installing the app on a new device.

Backup and restore

Open Settings → Data Management → Export Backup. Restoring a backup replaces the app's current data, so verify the selected file and preserve a second copy before continuing. Keep backups even when iCloud sync is available.

Read-only team sharing

A team owner can invite another BenchStats user to a read-only shared team. The owner publishes team updates manually, and invited users retrieve the latest published copy. This is separate from private device sync and is not live staff collaboration.

LiveBarn

Use your own LiveBarn account for video access. BenchStats does not store LiveBarn passwords and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LiveBarn.

Privacy
